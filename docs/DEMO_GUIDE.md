# Demo Guide

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Keep the prefilled local sandbox URL.
3. Enter a scholarship goal and launch the workflow.
4. Open the AI Agent Workspace to review stages and audit output.
5. Use Eligibility Analyzer to extract requirements.
6. Use Document Checklist and Applicant Profile.
7. Prepare form values and validate them.
8. Open Human Approval Center and create or reject/approve a fill request.
9. Open the Browser Viewport to inspect the sandbox.
10. Confirm the final Submit application control is disabled.
